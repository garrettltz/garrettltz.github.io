2019 Game Jam Project

Housing Crisis 2

Housing Crisis 2 is a Unity game created during a 2019 game jam centered on the theme of what home means. Our team explored the idea that a home filled with constant conflict has little chance to remain stable or succeed.

Role Game Designer
Project Type Game Jam
Engine Unity
Back to Projects

Project Overview

The project was created under a limited game jam schedule and focused on communicating its theme through gameplay and visual design. The team used the idea of a struggling household to show how ongoing conflict can affect the stability of a home.

My Contribution

As a Game Designer, I collaborated with the team on the central concept, gameplay direction, and overall player experience. I also contributed to testing and iteration as the project was developed within the short game jam deadline.
