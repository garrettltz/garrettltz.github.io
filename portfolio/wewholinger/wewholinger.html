Senior Capstone Project

We Who Linger

We Who Linger is a large-scale Unity capstone project in which the player controls a guardian spirit defending a village while exploring the surrounding world for artifacts, memories, upgrades, and resources.

Role World Lead / Level Designer
Team Size 22 Team Members
Engine Unity
Back to Projects

Project Overview

The player alternates between defending the village and exploring nearby ruins. Combat is fast-paced and supernatural, while the exploration sections focus on atmosphere, environmental storytelling, and uncovering artifacts from an earlier age. Collected resources help procedurally generated villagers grow, fight, and survive as the game progresses toward a final battle.

My Contribution

As World Lead and Level Designer, I helped guide the design and construction of the game world. I coordinated with designers, artists, and programmers to maintain consistency across environments, support gameplay goals, and ensure that the world reinforced the project's tone and narrative.

I also contributed to environmental layout, iteration, testing, and team coordination throughout development. Working on a 22-person capstone team strengthened my communication, organization, and cross-functional collaboration skills.
